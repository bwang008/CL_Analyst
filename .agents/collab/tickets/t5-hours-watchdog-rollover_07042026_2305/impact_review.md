### V3. Roll tolerance semantics — **PASS; 0.001 sanity-checked and ACKed (Q2)**
- Verified all three sites (`data_manager.py:271,654,947`) use `abs(ratio − 1.0) > _ROLL_PRICE_TOLERANCE` — **ratio-space**; the `:132` "$" comment is wrong as the audit says.
- **Direction confirmed:** BELOW tolerance = roll detected but NO adjustment applied (cache not adjusted, ledger not scaled, roll_history not appended) while `last_front_month` is still updated → the small real gap is silently swallowed forever. The tolerance is a **noise floor**, not a detection gate — detection is the front-month string change; `_compute_roll_ratio` only runs on a detected roll. So LOWERING it to 0.001 makes real ES (~20-70 bps) / GC (~50-100 bps) gaps get correctly applied; it cannot cause spurious adjustments outside a genuine contract change. Measurement noise is the overlap-median of same-timestamp closes from the same feed (≈0; occasional IBKR bar revisions are damped by the 50-bar median) — 10 bps floor is comfortably above it. **0.001 is NOT too low; the failure mode of "too low" is misapplied noise, and the noise is ~0.** CL/MCL pinned at 0.01 = today's exact behavior.
- Coupling worth minuting: 0.001 is safe partly BECAUSE the C2 namespace fix ships in the same change (phantom detections with ≈1.0 noisy ratios die before the lower floor can matter). Ship them together, as proposed.
